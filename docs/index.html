How we count

These numbers come only from phones whose owner tapped "Yes, I'm 18 or older" when the app asked to share usage data. Many readers say no, and they are not here. So every number is a floor: the truth is at least this much.

We use Google Analytics. For a phone that said yes, it sends a random ID for that install, the country, the app's language, when the app is opened, how long a reader reads, how many chapters are finished and whether the doorway is on, and the rest of the list above. It never sends a name, a city, other apps, or which books, chapters or verses were read. We never know what you read.

A "reader" is a phone, not a person. A shared phone counts once. If someone reinstalls the app in the same week they may count twice, which is why we show readers per week and per month and never a grand total of readers.

We hide any figure that fewer than 50 phones stand behind, and we round every number down. Rates such as "about 4 in 10" need 500 phones. "About N verses" is chapters multiplied by 26, the Bible's average; we do not count verses.

Installs, phones in use, countries and continents come from Google Play, which counts every install without knowing who anyone is. A country is counted only once it has 50 installs. We publish how many countries, never which ones, because for some readers that could be dangerous.

Per-phone records are deleted after about four months; only the totals stay. The page is updated each Monday after a person has checked it. If we get a number wrong we say so under Corrections.

The growth is not ours. "I planted, Apollos watered, but God gave the growth." (1 Corinthians 3:6-7)
